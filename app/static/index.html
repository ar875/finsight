    How it was tested

    The answers were checked against a hand-reviewed test set of real questions, each traced back to a page in the PDFs. Numbers are scored exactly, with units converted (₹ thousand, lakh, crore, million, billion), so a missing "thousand" counts as wrong.

    Search combines keyword matching and meaning-based matching, narrows to the companies and years in the question, and re-ranks the best 30 passages before six are sent to the model. When no passage matches well, the model is not asked at all.

    Answers come only from the listed documents and can still be wrong; check the cited page before relying on a figure.
